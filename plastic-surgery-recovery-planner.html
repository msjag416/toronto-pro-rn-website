Why a recovery nurse made a plastic surgery recovery planner

I've recovered plastic surgery patients in Toronto for 14 years, and the first week brings the same questions every time. How much drainage is too much? When is the next pain pill due? Is it normal for one side to be more swollen than the other? Patients go home with a bag of garments, a printed sheet from the clinic, and a family member who hasn't emptied a JP drain before.

On the first night, a nurse writes all of this down for your surgeon. After that, it's up to you and the person looking after you. The planner gives you one place to write it down as it happens, so you aren't guessing at 2 a.m. and you can bring the facts to your follow-up appointment.

Who the recovery planner is for

The planner is for anyone having breast augmentation, a breast lift or reduction, a BBL, a tummy tuck, liposuction, a mommy makeover, a facelift, eyelid surgery or a rhinoplasty. It works wherever you have your surgery and recover, in Toronto, elsewhere in Canada, the United States or abroad.

It's also for the partner, friend or family member looking after you. The drain output log and the medication record tell them exactly what to write down and when, and the "When to Call Your Surgeon or 911" part tells them what to watch for.

Plastic surgery recovery planner questions

Is the plastic surgery recovery planner a printed book?

No. It's a fillable PDF you download from Etsy as soon as you pay. You can type into it on a computer or tablet, or print the pages at home and fill them in by hand. Nothing is mailed to you.

Which surgeries does the recovery planner work for?

Breast augmentation, breast lift, breast reduction, BBL, tummy tuck, liposuction, mommy makeover, facelift, eyelid surgery, rhinoplasty and more. The procedure checklist has a line in each section to type in a surgery that isn't listed.

Does the planner replace my surgeon's instructions?

No. Follow your surgeon's written post-op orders first. The planner is where you write down what happens each day, such as drain output, swelling, pain and medication times, so your surgeon has the facts at your follow-up appointment.

Can I use the planner if I'm not having surgery in Toronto?

Yes. The planner works wherever you have surgery and recover, in Canada, the United States or anywhere else. Only the Toronto ProRN nursing service is limited to Toronto and the GTA.

What sizes does the recovery planner come in?

You get US Letter size (8.5 x 11 inches, 23 pages) and A4 size (22 pages), with 863 boxes you can type into or tick.
