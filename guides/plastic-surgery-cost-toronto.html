How Much Does Plastic Surgery Cost in Toronto?

2026 price guide: tummy tucks, breast implants, liposuction, BBLs, facelifts and the recovery expenses patients often forget.

JoAnne Gomez, RN

27+ years ICU · 14+ years plastic surgery recovery · June 2026 · Updated November 2, 2026

Thinking about plastic surgery in Toronto? You’ve probably noticed that one clinic advertises a tummy tuck for around $12,000, while another quotes closer to $19,000. It can be hard to compare the numbers, especially when some fees are only available after a consultation. This guide puts published Toronto clinic prices in one place, explains why quotes differ, and helps you plan for the care you’ll need once you leave the operating room.

Quick answer: Published prices run from a few thousand dollars for limited procedures to $60,500 or more for some facelifts. A larger operation or a combination of procedures will cost more. The figures below are examples and planning ranges, not a citywide statistical average or an individual surgical quote. Ask every clinic whether HST is included.

Why does Lipo 360 cost so much more at some clinics?

Lipo 360 treats several areas around the waist and torso rather than a single small fat deposit. The fee depends on the number of areas, the expected fat removal, the time in the operating room, and any contour corrections. For perspective, Canadian Plastic Surgery Centre advertises Lipo 360 starting at about $15,500, while TCSI lists $19,500+ and explains that the final cost can rise significantly. A combined BBL with Lipo 360 at TCSI starts at $26,100+. These examples explain why a single “average Toronto price” can be misleading.

The recovery can be more involved than patients expect. Swelling, bruising and wound drainage may be substantial. Extensive circumferential liposuction can also be associated with changes in circulating fluid volume and blood pressure. Your surgeon should explain what is expected, what is concerning, and what support you need at home or at your hotel.

How much does a tummy tuck cost in Toronto?

A mini, full and extended tummy tuck are different operations, so the prices differ too. A full abdominoplasty can include removal of excess abdominal skin and repair of separated abdominal muscles. TCSI currently lists $13,300+ for a mini tummy tuck and $15,300-$19,000+ for a full tummy tuck, without liposuction. Other clinics may package services differently. Ask whether muscle repair, drain supplies, garments and follow-up appointments are included.

Recovery is another part of the budget. Getting out of bed, walking to the washroom and managing drains may require assistance during the first days. If you live alone or you’re travelling to Toronto, work out who will help you before you book surgery.

How much does a Brazilian butt lift (BBL) cost?

At TCSI, the advertised BBL price starts at $14,500 with one liposuction area and $26,100+ with Lipo 360. That difference matters because the liposuction component can be extensive. A BBL also has distinct safety considerations. Ask your surgeon about the fat-injection plane, use of real-time ultrasound guidance, anesthesia and facility accreditation. Follow your surgeon's restrictions on sitting and lying directly on the buttocks after surgery.

If you’re staying in a Toronto hotel after a BBL, plan how you’ll get in and out of bed, use the bathroom and travel without violating your surgeon's positioning instructions. A recovery nurse can help with monitoring, prescribed garments, drainage records and safe mobility within the surgical team’s plan.

How much does breast surgery cost in Toronto?

Breast augmentation pricing depends on implant type, breast anatomy and whether a lift is required. At TCSI, saline augmentation starts at $10,500 and silicone at $12,800. Adding a lift increases the starting price to $15,100 or $17,100 respectively. The clinic lists breast lift alone at $10,600-$12,100+ and non-OHIP breast reduction at $12,100+. Implants can require future imaging, removal or revision; ask about long-term costs as well as the initial operation.

Some breast reductions may qualify for OHIP coverage when Ontario's medical criteria and authorization requirements are met. Cosmetic preference alone doesn’t establish eligibility. Discuss this with your physician before paying privately.

What about facelifts, rhinoplasty and eyelid surgery?

Facial surgery prices vary because a mini facelift, full facelift, deep-plane procedure and combined neck lift may involve different operative plans. TCSI publishes a facelift range of $22,000-$60,500+, showing that a generic $15,000-$33,000 estimate won’t describe every Toronto practice. Rhinoplasty is often advertised around $10,900-$19,000+ at selected clinics. TCSI lists upper eyelid surgery at $6,700+ and lower eyelid surgery at $10,800+. Always verify whether the quote covers one area or a combination.

After facial and neck surgery, rapidly increasing swelling, bleeding or breathing difficulty requires urgent medical assessment. Your surgeon should provide clear after-hours instructions, and should advise whether monitored recovery or overnight nursing care is appropriate.

Does Ontario charge HST on cosmetic plastic surgery?

Generally, yes. Purely cosmetic surgical procedures and related services are subject to Ontario's 13% HST. Exceptions can apply when services are performed for qualifying medical or reconstructive purposes. Ask for an itemized written quote showing whether tax has already been included.

Costs patients sometimes forget

Consultations and deposits. Check whether the consultation fee can be credited toward the operation and whether the surgical deposit is refundable. Anesthesia and facility fees. Confirm whether these are included. Garments and supplies. A faja, surgical bra, dressings or an extra garment may cost more. Medication. Ask about prescription costs. Travel. If you're coming from outside Toronto, include hotels, transportation and a possible longer local stay. Help after discharge. Determine who will assist you during the first night and who can recognize changes that need medical attention.

How much does a private plastic surgery recovery nurse cost in Toronto?

At Toronto ProRN, private post-operative nursing is priced at $85 per hour, with an eight-hour minimum booking. ICU-trained Registered Nurses experienced in plastic surgery recovery provide care in suitable homes, hotels and other recovery accommodations in Toronto. Transportation or holiday charges may apply, and coverage depends on availability.

Depending on your surgeon's orders and your individual needs, a recovery nurse may monitor blood pressure, heart rate and oxygen saturation; assess pain and nausea; help you move safely; assist with a prescribed compression garment; and observe and record output from JP or Hemovac drains. If a saline lock remains for prescribed follow-up treatment, care must follow the authorized treatment plan and nursing requirements. Your nurse can also recognize concerning findings and contact the surgeon or emergency services when necessary.

Nursing observation doesn’t eliminate surgical risks or replace a hospital, surgeon or emergency department. But for a patient who is alone, travelling or recovering from extensive surgery, arranging qualified help before the operation can make the first night much easier to manage.

When you set your budget, leave room for help. Can you recover alone after a tummy tuck, Lipo 360, BBL or mommy makeover? No. After major plastic surgery, you’ll need someone with you during the initial recovery period. Plan for a family member, a friend or a private nurse to be there before you book your surgery date.

Should you choose the least expensive plastic surgeon?

A lower price doesn’t always mean the same procedure or the same included services. Check the surgeon's qualifications through the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario register, confirm the facility and anesthesia arrangements, and ask who handles urgent problems after hours. Request a written quote listing the procedure, surgeon, taxes and all expected extras. Then compare the same information across clinics.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I budget for a tummy tuck in Toronto? Roughly $11,000-$19,000+ is a useful initial planning range, but liposuction, HST and additional care may increase your total. TCSI quotes $15,300-$19,000+ for a full tummy tuck without liposuction.

How much does BBL with Lipo 360 cost? TCSI advertises this combination starting at $26,100+. Your personalized quote can be higher.

Are published online prices guaranteed? No. Clinics typically quote after a consultation, and some advertised figures are starting fees only.

Can I recover in a Toronto hotel? Some patients can, but confirm the arrangement with your surgeon, including transportation, a responsible adult after anesthesia, positioning restrictions and any nursing needs.

Can I finance plastic surgery? Some clinics use third-party lenders. Compare interest, loan length, fees and total repayment, not only the monthly amount.
