Exactly what's included

Clinical care, logistics and privacy, in one fee.

Every package includes everything below. Your nurse follows your surgeon's written post-operative orders throughout.

01

Surgical discharge and suite setup

Clinic pick-up and handover
Your ICU Registered Nurse meets you inside the surgical clinic and receives the clinical handover directly from the operating or recovery room team.
Prescription collection
Your nurse collects and checks your post-op prescriptions, antibiotics and pain medication before you reach the hotel.
Suite preparation
Your hotel room or residence is set up with waterproof bedding protection, positioning pillows, elevation wedges and surgical supplies.
02

Clinical care with ICU oversight

Continuous, awake Registered Nurse care
One-on-one care from a College of Nurses of Ontario Registered Nurse with ICU and plastic surgery recovery experience. On longer packages, nurses work planned shifts so the nurse with you is always alert.
Vital signs and airway monitoring
Blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen saturation, temperature and breathing tracked through the night.
Watching for complications
Early recognition of hematoma, blood clots, fluid loss after liposuction and breathing problems, with your surgeon called right away when something changes. What to expect in the first 72 hours →

JP and Hemovac drains stripped, emptied, measured and logged as your surgeon orders. What JP drain fluid colours mean →

Questions

Before you book.

Can my assistant, family office or hotel concierge book for me?

Yes. Many concierge bookings are arranged by a personal assistant, family office or hotel concierge. JoAnne still speaks with you before the booking is final, because your nurse needs to know your procedure and your surgeon.

Can you sign a non-disclosure agreement?

Yes. On request, Toronto ProRN and your attending nurse sign an NDA before your first shift. Every concierge booking also follows the no-photo rule inside your suite.

Do you clear me to fly home?

Clearance to fly comes from your surgeon. On the 48-hour package your nurse documents your flight readiness, including vital signs, drains, walking and pain control, and sends it to your surgeon to support that decision.

Is one nurse with me for 36 or 48 hours?

You're never without a nurse. On the longer packages our nurses work planned shifts, so whoever is with you is awake and alert. JoAnne introduces each nurse to you before they arrive.

Which currency do I pay in?

If you live in Canada, you pay in Canadian dollars by Interac e-Transfer or card. If you live outside Canada, you pay in US dollars through a secure Stripe card payment link sent once your booking is confirmed.

What if I need more time than my package?

Extra hours can be added at the same flat concierge rate of $195 USD an hour, arranged with JoAnne ahead of time.
