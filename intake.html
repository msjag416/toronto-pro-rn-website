Privacy notice

Last updated:

Toronto ProRN protects the privacy and confidentiality of your personal health information under the Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA) of Ontario and applicable Canadian privacy law.

This form collects only what's needed to plan safe nursing care and prepare your quote: your contact details, surgery and discharge plans, recovery address, who will be with you, the care you need and yes or no health screening answers. Detailed medical information is collected privately by phone, not through this form.

Your answers are saved in this browser on this device while you fill out the form, so you can come back later. They are erased from this device when you submit, or when you choose "Start over". To check whether your recovery address is in the fee-free zone, the street address, city and postal code are looked up with OpenStreetMap's map search. Nothing else is sent to it.

Your information is never sold, shared or used for marketing unless you opt in to updates. Clinical records are kept as required by the College of Nurses of Ontario.

To ask what information is held about you, or to request a correction or deletion, email prorninc@gmail.com.
