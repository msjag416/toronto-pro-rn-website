International patients · Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Travelling to Toronto for plastic surgery? Recover in your hotel with an ICU nurse beside you.

Patients fly to Toronto from the United States, the Caribbean, the Gulf, the United Kingdom and across Canada for cosmetic surgery. Most recover far from home, in a hotel room with no one checking on them. Our ICU-trained Registered Nurses stay awake through your first nights after surgery, in your hotel, rental or family home, so you are never alone when it matters most.

How it works

Planning your recovery nurse from another country

  1. 1

    Book your surgeon, then fill out the intake form

    Once your surgery date is set, complete the intake form from your phone or computer. It covers your surgery, discharge plan, hotel and nursing dates, and shows your cost estimate. Your answers save as you go, so you can finish it later.

  2. 2

    Talk with us by WhatsApp or video call

    JoAnne Gomez, RN, the founder of Toronto ProRN, answers personally and matches you with your nurse. WhatsApp and video calls work from anywhere in the world, at no cost to you.

  3. 3

    Confirm with a deposit, paid by card from your country

    A 25% deposit confirms your booking, through a secure Stripe card payment link. The balance is due 48 hours before your first shift.

  4. 4

    Your nurse meets you at your hotel after surgery

    When you are discharged, your nurse is waiting at your recovery address, goes through your surgeon's written orders with you, and stays awake all night monitoring your vital signs, drains, pain, breathing and first steps.

  5. 5

    Fly home when your surgeon clears you

    You leave with a written record of every night of care for your surgeon and your doctor at home.

Where to stay

Choosing a hotel for your recovery in Toronto

Many of Toronto's luxury hotels, and the cosmetic surgery clinics around Yorkville and downtown, sit inside our fee-free zone: south of Bloor Street, east of Dufferin Street, west of the Don Valley and north of Queens Quay. Recovering inside the red line on the map means no transportation fee for your nurse.

When you choose a room, look for an elevator, a bed that is easy to get in and out of, a reclining chair if you have had a tummy tuck or BBL, and a short drive to your surgeon's clinic for follow-up appointments. Let the hotel know a private nurse will be visiting overnight.

No transportation fee inside the red line. Outside it, a $30 fee applies per shift. Map: Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Travelling from

Coming to Toronto from your part of the world

From the United States

Toronto is a short flight from New York, Chicago, Boston, Detroit and most major US cities. US citizens need only a valid passport to visit Canada, and you pay in US dollars, so there is no currency conversion on your card. Our nurses follow your Toronto surgeon's orders and give you a written record for your doctor back home.

From the Caribbean and Bermuda

Nonstop flights link Toronto with Grand Cayman, Bermuda and islands across the Caribbean, including the Bahamas, Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago and Barbados. Many Caribbean patients have family in Toronto to stay with. JoAnne Gomez, RN, our founder, has worked as an ICU nurse in Grand Cayman.

From the UAE and the Gulf

Emirates and Air Canada fly nonstop between Dubai and Toronto every day, a flight of about 14 hours. Patients from the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Kuwait often recover in a hotel suite with family nearby. We provide female nurses by default, with complete privacy and discretion.

From the United Kingdom and Europe

Toronto has nonstop flights from London and major European cities. If you are combining surgery with a visit to family in Toronto, your nurse can care for you in a relative's home, with written handover notes for whoever looks after you during the day.

From elsewhere in Canada

Patients fly in from Northern Ontario, the Atlantic provinces, the Prairies, British Columbia and the North because Toronto has more plastic surgeons. No passport needed. You pay in Canadian dollars, by Interac e-Transfer or credit card. Cosmetic recovery nursing is not covered by provincial health plans.

Before you book flights

Planning your stay, entry to Canada and payment

How long to stay

Your surgeon decides when it is safe to fly. Surgeons quoted by the American Society of Plastic Surgeons suggest waiting at least two weeks before long travel after surgery, because sitting still for hours raises the risk of blood clots. Book flights you can change, and plan around your follow-up appointments.

Entering Canada

US citizens need a valid passport. Most other visitors flying to Canada need an electronic travel authorization (eTA) or a visitor visa, depending on citizenship. Check the Government of Canada website well before you travel.

Paying from your country

Pay by credit card from any country through a secure Stripe payment link, sent once your booking is confirmed. Clients outside Canada pay in US dollars: $85 USD an hour with an 8-hour minimum, plus $30 USD per shift outside the fee-free zone. Clients in Canada pay the same amounts in Canadian dollars.

If your plans change

Cancel more than 14 days before your first shift and your deposit is refunded in full. If your surgeon postpones your surgery for a medical reason, your deposit moves to the new date. See the full cancellation policy.

Questions

What international patients ask before they book

Can I recover in a Toronto hotel after plastic surgery?

Yes. Most international patients recover in a hotel or short-term rental. An ICU-trained Registered Nurse meets you at your hotel after discharge and stays awake through the night monitoring your vital signs, drains, pain and breathing, and follows your surgeon's written post-operative orders.

How long should I stay in Toronto after surgery before flying home?

Your surgeon decides when it is safe for you to fly. Surgeons quoted by the American Society of Plastic Surgeons suggest waiting at least two weeks before long travel after surgery, because sitting still for hours raises the risk of blood clots. Plan your stay around your surgeon's follow-up appointments and leave extra days in case your surgeon wants to see you again.

Do I need a visa to travel to Canada for surgery?

It depends on your citizenship. US citizens need a valid passport. Most other visitors flying to Canada need either an electronic travel authorization (eTA) or a visitor visa. Check the Government of Canada website well before you book your flights.

How do I pay for my recovery nurse from outside Canada?

You can pay by credit card from any country through a secure Stripe payment link, sent once your booking is confirmed. Clients outside Canada pay in US dollars: $85 USD an hour with an 8-hour minimum. Clients in Canada pay $85 an hour in Canadian dollars. A 25% deposit confirms your booking.

Will the nurse pick me up from the surgical clinic?

No. Toronto ProRN does not provide transportation. Your nurse meets you at your hotel or recovery address. Arrange a car service, taxi or companion to bring you from the clinic, following your surgeon's discharge instructions.

What if my surgery date changes?

If your surgeon postpones or cancels your surgery for a medical reason, your deposit moves to the new date, or is refunded if no new date is set. Cancellations more than 14 days before your first nursing shift receive a full deposit refund.
